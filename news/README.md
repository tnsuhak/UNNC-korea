# UNNC Korea News

This directory contains localized Korean news articles selected from official UNNC sources.

- Preserve the exact official source URL and source publication date.
- Use review-first publishing through a feature branch and Netlify Deploy Preview.
- Prefer original Korean summaries focused on prospective students and parents.
- Do not publish routine ceremonies or low-value notices unless they have clear admissions, programme, career, recognition, or student-outcome value.
- Keep article images empty by default unless the central policy is explicitly changed.
